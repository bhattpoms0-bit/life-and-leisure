separately in `src/data/domesticHolidays.js`; the new routes are
`/domestic-holidays`, `/domestic-holidays/kerala` and
`/domestic-holidays/kerala/:slug`.

Base INR 29999, Signature INR 44999 and Premium INR 74999 are indicative,
provisional land-only commercial targets per person, not confirmed selling
prices. The basis is at least four adults sharing twin/double accommodation.
Two-person/family prices require a separate quotation. Dates, properties, room
categories, supplier availability and charges require confirmation. No taxes,
departure dates, hotel confirmations, reviews, partnerships or health outcomes
are invented. No payment or instant-booking workflow is introduced.

are represented separately. Premium Day 3 retains optional participation in a
prearranged wellness session; Day 6 is the second session. Two sessions are
included per consenting adult, subject to qualified-provider screening and
availability. Signature includes one prebooked 60-minute relaxation massage,
subject to screening, consent and scheduling. Operational details and specified
dining arrangements must be confirmed in the final quotation.
